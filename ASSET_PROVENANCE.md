## Notebook presentation and scientific figures

The five PNGs under `notebooks/assets/archive-window/` were created for the project's approved Archive Window presentation: four compact research-file badges and a lavender section divider. The badge lettering was rendered using the existing Jersey 10 font. These raster presentation additions are distinct from the scientific figures and the arcade-room artwork.

Scientific figure provenance remains in the included plotting code, including [portfolio_visuals.py](src/portfolio_visuals.py) and [explore_data.py](src/explore_data.py). Saved figures, chart legends and scientific values were retained unchanged in this public snapshot. This provenance statement does not grant reuse rights to underlying third-party observations.
